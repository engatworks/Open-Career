**Not legal advice. Not a binding agreement or sufficient for public launch. Must be reviewed by qualified counsel for the operator's identity, applicable law, privacy and consumer rules.** Proposed terms version: mvp-2026-10-19-v1.

## Draft terms
**Service:** Open Career is job-search assistance software, not an employer, employment agency, immigration attorney, visa sponsor or recruiter. It offers research, application preparation, outreach and records with user-enabled third-party tools. It does not guarantee job offers, interviews, valid vacancies, employer responses, mailbox placement or uninterrupted availability.

**Truthful information:** The user provides and must keep accurate identity, qualifications, CV, employment dates, legal eligibility and application answers. The service may make mistakes and must stop for material contradictions or uncertain attestations rather than fabricate data. The user can inspect/export submissions and has responsibility for the accuracy of representations made on their behalf.

**Separate opt-in agency grants:** After seeing these Terms and the Privacy Notice, a user may independently authorize job research, application-email reading, initial outbound email, employer-thread replies, follow-ups, portal-account creation, encrypted secret storage, form submission and application record updates. Enabled categories may run autonomously within logged limits and documented provider permissions without separate pre-send approval where the host allows. Every permission is versioned, auditable, individually revocable and limited to the authenticated user's job search. Nothing permits bypassing a host approval or employer restrictions.

**External services:** ChatGPT, Claude, LinkedIn, Apollo, Gmail and employer ATS portals impose independent permissions, policies, plan limits and account-security measures. The user is responsible for their own accounts; software will not evade paid credits, blocklists, access controls or site-imposed verification. Sites and AI hosts may deny, pause, rate-limit or disable actions.

**Secrets and personal information:** Applicant portals can contain sensitive identity documents, contact details and employment history. Access credentials are secrets. Distinct passwords should be generated by default and stored only in private encrypted storage under user control; never in a public repository, plain logs, CSVs or prompts. The operator must publish a separate privacy notice specifying collected information, recipient processors, retention, export/deletion options, transfers and breach handling.

**Limitations:** To the maximum extent lawfully permitted, software is provided 'as is' and 'as available' without a promise of fitness, accuracy, availability, employment results or non-interruption. Any liability limitation, exclusions, warranties, indemnification or dispute-resolution mechanism must be drafted for the actual legal operator and governing jurisdiction and cannot waive non-waivable rights. An open-source license doesn't replace end-user terms or data-protection obligations.

**Suspension and deletion:** The operator may suspend abuse or compromised access. Users can revoke permissions, stop scheduled tasks and request account export/deletion subject to a published retention policy and mandatory laws.

## Acceptance UX (must be implemented before external release)
1. Prominent linked Terms AND Privacy Notice; unselected affirmative 'I agree' checkbox; no assumed acceptance by mere site use.
2. Individual unchecked permission toggles for outbound mail, replies, follow-ups, account creation, form submission and encrypted credential storage. Research can have separate read consent. Display that actions may be irreversible.
3. Accept button is clicked by the human, never by the agent. The authenticated server saves user ID, timestamp, terms hash/version, consent signal and selected scopes, with revocation history.
4. On every MCP write, enforce the current scope and account ownership in server code. UI-only disclaimers are not an authorization system.
5. Before public launch: qualified legal review, actual D1 grant enforcement, OAuth/identity isolation, vault security and deletion/export tests.
